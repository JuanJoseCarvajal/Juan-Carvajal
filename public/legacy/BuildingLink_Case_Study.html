BuildingLink

BuildingLink is the industry’s most trusted property management software. It helps property managers, developers, and condominium boards worldwide enhance the resident experience while streamlining maintenance and operations.

Problem

A deep understanding of the BuildingLink web application is essential during the discovery phase. To ensure a thorough evaluation, a rapid UX, UI, and Accessibility audit will be conducted, providing a comprehensive assessment of the platform’s features, functionalities, and dependencies.

This accelerated audit will equip all stakeholders with critical insights into the application’s current architecture, enabling data-driven decision-making. By identifying key opportunities and challenges early on, the audit will support a well-informed and efficient migration strategy, minimizing risks and ensuring a seamless transition.

The core objective is to transition the BuildingLink app to a modern front-end framework, optimizing performance, maintainability, and long-term scalability. This upgrade will future-proof the platform, ensuring adaptability to evolving user expectations and industry advancements.

In parallel, a robust and scalable design system will be established, integrating best practices in accessibility and usability. This system will drive consistency across all interfaces, streamline development, and elevate the overall user experience for both residents and administrators.

Solution

The initial phase focuses on conducting a comprehensive analysis of the app’s current state by mapping “As-Is” scenarios based on user flows. This process involves systematically documenting each feature and functionality to establish a detailed reference framework for the migration strategy.

Simultaneously, critical pain points and opportunities for enhancement across UX, UI, and accessibility will be identified. These insights will form the foundation for refining the user experience, ensuring a more intuitive, efficient, and inclusive design that aligns with best practices and user expectations.

Discovery
Framing the Problem

To ensure alignment in this project, it is essential to build upon a deep understanding of the product in its current state. This involves gathering comprehensive information on its architecture, including user types, workflows, data structures, visual design, functionalities, and components.

By analyzing these elements, we can create a well-informed foundation for identifying challenges, opportunities, and areas for improvement, setting the stage for a successful transformation.

Information architecture

Information architecture organizes content to help users understand where they are in a product and where the information they want is.

When users find a product easy to use, it means they can find what they’re looking for, quickly and intuitively. This isn’t by chance; it’s by design.

Information architecture also defines the overall structure of the app or the site. It also provides a high-level view of a product.

UX/UI Audit

We performed an in-depth analysis of your interface to assess its strengths and areas for improvement. This evaluation measured the extent to which your platform adheres to industry best practices, usability heuristics, and user interface (UI) principles, ensuring that the findings are actionable.

Our analysis compared the existing design against established usability guidelines, research-based UX principles, and our expertise in conducting usability testing. This allowed us to identify and prioritize key usability challenges that impact user experience and efficiency.

To facilitate effective decision-making, we categorized the findings based on severity, helping your team systematically address the most critical usability concerns.

User Flows Mapping

During the discovery phase, the Business Analyst (BA) and UX teams conducted a comprehensive survey of the modules defined by the business. This research led to the development of detailed flowcharts that visually represent the primary functionalities of these modules, ensuring clarity and alignment across teams. These flowcharts serve as a foundational reference for optimizing user interactions and improving system navigation.

Accessibility Report

The Web is designed to be inclusive, ensuring equal access and usability for individuals regardless of their hardware, software, language, location, or ability. A well-designed digital experience embraces accessibility principles to accommodate users with diverse needs, including those with visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive impairments.

By meeting accessibility standards, your platform can enhance usability for all users while complying with global regulations such as WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines). This approach not only fosters inclusivity but also improves overall user satisfaction and engagement.

Result

Through a research-driven UX/UI transformation, BuildingLink is now positioned as a more intuitive, efficient, and accessible property management platform.

By leveraging best-in-class UX practices, streamlined workflows, and a modern design system, we’ve helped future-proof the platform while improving the daily experiences of residents, property managers, and service staff.

The redesign not only elevates the user experience but also optimizes operations, ensuring BuildingLink remains the industry leader in property management technology.
